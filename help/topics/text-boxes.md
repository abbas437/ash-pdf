# Adding and formatting text boxes

The **Text** tool (<kbd>T</kbd>) types new text onto a page in a box that you can format, frame, move and resize. It does not change the document's original text: for that, see [Editing existing text](#topic:edit-text) and [Replacing text](#topic:replace-text).

## Add a text box

1. Choose **Text** on the toolbar, or press <kbd>T</kbd>. [Show me](#showme:tool:text)
2. Click on the page for a box 200 pt wide, or drag to draw a box of your own size.
3. Type. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line; the text wraps at the box edge and the box grows to fit.
4. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> or click outside the box to finish. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the box.

A box left empty is not added. To edit a box again, double-click it with the **Select** or **Text** tool.

> **Tip:** Press <kbd>T</kbd> (or click **Text**) again to go back to the Select tool.

## Options bar

While the Text tool is active, or when text boxes are selected, the options bar shows the text settings. [Show me](#showme:panel:options-bar)

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Font | Helvetica, Times or Courier |
| Size | 4 to 144 pt |
| B / I / U / S | Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough |
| Colour | Text colour |
| Alignment | Align left, centre or right |
| Border | None, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 or 3 pt, with a border colour |
| Fill | Tick to give the box a background colour |
| Margin | Inner margin between the frame and the text, 0 to 36 pt |

Changes apply to the box you are typing in, to the selected boxes, and to new boxes.

## Format part of the text

Select words inside the box, then use the options bar, the floating format bar above the box, or the keyboard. With no words selected, the format applies to the whole box. [Show me](#showme:bar:text-format)

| Keys | Format |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> | Bold |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> | Italic |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>U</kbd> | Underline |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>X</kbd> | Strikethrough |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd> | Undo / redo inside the box |

The floating bar also has quick colours: **Black**, **Red**, **Rev. comment (blue)**, **Green** and **Orange**, plus **Custom colour**.

### Right-click menu

Right-click inside the box for **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**, **Select all**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, and the **Font**, **Size** (8 to 36, or **Custom…**) and **Colour** submenus. Right-clicking a word with nothing selected selects that word first.

## Pictures in a text box

A text box can hold PNG or JPEG pictures in line with the text:

- **Paste** a copied picture with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>, or
- click **Picture** on the floating bar, or right-click and choose **Insert picture…**.

A picture wider than the box is scaled down to fit. Click a picture to select it; right-click it and choose **Picture size** (25%, 50%, 100% or **Fit width**) to resize it. Press <kbd>Delete</kbd> to remove a selected picture.

## Move and resize

- **While typing:** drag the thin frame just outside the box to move it.
- **When selected:** with the Select tool, drag the box to move it, or drag one of its 8 handles to resize. The text re-wraps to the new width; a height you drag larger is kept.

See [Selecting, grouping and the clipboard](#topic:select-group-clipboard).

> **Note:** Characters outside the standard PDF fonts cannot be saved and appear as "?". A warning shows under the box when you type one.

## What other PDF readers show

Text boxes are saved as standard PDF text-box (FreeText) annotations with their own appearance, so Acrobat, Foxit and browsers show them as you see them here and can move or delete them. If you edit such a box in another program, formatting or pictures that program does not support may be lost. To fix boxes permanently into the page, see [Flattening annotations](#topic:flatten-annotations).
