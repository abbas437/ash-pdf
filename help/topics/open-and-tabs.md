## Open a PDF

1. Choose **File ▸ Open…** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>. [Show me](#showme:btn:open)
2. Select one or more PDF files and click **Open**. Each file opens in its own tab.

Other ways to open:

- **Drag and drop**: drop PDF files on the start screen or on the tab bar to open them as new tabs. Only PDF files can be opened.
- **File ▸ Recent files…** reopens a file you used recently.
- **File ▸ New window** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>) opens an empty window; **File ▸ Open in new window…** opens a file in a new window.

> **Tip:** Dropping a PDF on the *pages* of an open document inserts its pages there instead. A line shows where they go and the hint reads “Insert at page N · drop on the tab bar to open”.

## Switch between tabs

- Click a tab, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> (next) and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> (previous).
- With a tab focused, <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move to the neighbouring tab.

## Reorder tabs

Drag a tab sideways and drop it in its new place (<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels), or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd> to move the current tab left or right.

## Close tabs

- Click the **×** on the tab, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd>, or choose **File ▸ Close tab**.
- Middle-click a tab, or press <kbd>Delete</kbd> while the tab has focus.
- Right-click a tab for **Close**, **Close other tabs**, **Close tabs to the left**, **Close tabs to the right** and **Close all tabs**.

If a document has unsaved changes you are asked whether to save it, discard the changes or cancel. When you close several tabs at once, **Cancel** stops the rest from closing.

## Copy pages between documents

Drag page thumbnails from the sidebar onto another tab. Hold them over the tab for a moment to switch to it, then drop them on its pages to insert copies there (“Insert copies at page N”), or drop them on the tab itself to add them at the end (“Append N pages to …”).

## Encrypted files

An encrypted PDF opens read-only. A banner says **This document is encrypted: viewing and printing only (editing is not supported).** and the status bar shows **Encrypted · read-only**. Dropped files open in a new tab instead of being inserted.
