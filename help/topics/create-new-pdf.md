# Create a new blank PDF

**File ▸ New PDF…** creates an empty document of the page size, orientation and number of pages you choose, ready for pictures, text and shapes. [Show me the File menu](#showme:menu:File).

## Steps

1. Choose **File ▸ New PDF…** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>), or click **New PDF…** on the start screen.
2. Pick a **Page size**. For **Custom…**, enter the **Width** and **Height** in millimetres (25.4 to 5080 mm each).
3. Pick the **Orientation**: **Portrait** (upright) or **Landscape** (long side across).
4. Enter the **Number of pages** (1 to 500).
5. Click **Create**. The document opens in a new tab named **Untitled 1.pdf** (then Untitled 2, and so on).

Your choices are remembered for the next new PDF.

## Page sizes

| Size | Portrait width × height |
| --- | --- |
| A4 | 210 × 297 mm |
| A3 | 297 × 420 mm |
| A5 | 148 × 210 mm |
| Letter | 215.9 × 279.4 mm (8.5 × 11 in) |
| Legal | 215.9 × 355.6 mm (8.5 × 14 in) |
| Custom | 25.4 to 5080 mm per side |

## Saving

The new document is not saved until you save it. An untouched blank tab closes without asking. **File ▸ Save** asks where to save it and suggests the tab name, for example `Untitled 1.pdf`.
