# Stamps

A stamp is a labelled mark such as APPROVED or DRAFT that you place on a page. Stamps are annotations, so you can select, move, restyle and delete them afterwards.

## Place a stamp

1. Press <kbd>S</kbd> or choose **Tools ▸ Stamp**. [Show me](#showme:tool:stamp)
2. In the options bar, click the stamp button (tooltip **Choose a stamp**) and pick a stamp from the palette. [Show me](#showme:panel:options-bar)
3. Optionally set the colour (Green, Red, Blue or Orange), the stamp shape, the Rotation (-45 to 45 degrees) and **Add date**, which places a second "DATE:" stamp under the first.
4. Click the page to place the stamp at its default size, or drag to draw it at the size you want.

## Kinds of stamp

The palette has three tabs.

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| Standard | 20 ready-made stamps, such as APPROVED, APPROVED AS NOTED, REJECTED, DRAFT, FINAL, CONFIDENTIAL and VOID |
| Dynamic | APPROVED, REVIEWED, RECEIVED, REJECTED, REVISED and COMPLETED, with a second line such as "by A. Example · 2026-10-07" filled in when you place them |
| Custom | Your own stamps |

For dynamic stamps, choose the Date format and tick **Author**, **Date** and **Time** as needed. The author comes from your [Author name](#topic:author-name).

## Create your own stamps

On the Custom tab:

1. Choose **Create stamp…** to make a text stamp: enter one line of text, then choose Colour, Shape and whether it has a Border.
2. Choose **From image…** to make an image stamp from a PNG or JPEG. Tick **Remove light background** and adjust the Threshold if the picture has a white background.
3. Use the pencil icon to rename a custom stamp, or the cross to delete it.

## Default stamp shape

Stamps are rectangles unless you choose otherwise. To change the shape for new stamps, open **Edit ▸ Preferences…**, go to **Annotations** and set **Default stamp shape** to Rectangle, Rounded, Circle or Ellipse. See [Preferences](#topic:preferences).

> **Tip:** To place a picture or logo that is not a reusable stamp, see [Images](#topic:images).
