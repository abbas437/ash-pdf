# Crop and resize pages

Both commands are on the **Tools** menu and the **Pages** toolbar button. [Show me](#showme:btn:pages)

## Crop pages

Cropping hides the area outside a box. Margins are trimmed from each page's own edges, as the page is displayed.

> **Important:** The hidden area stays in the file. Cropping is not redaction. To remove content permanently, see [Redaction](#topic:redaction).

1. Choose **Tools ▸ Crop pages…**
2. Enter the **Top**, **Right**, **Bottom** and **Left** margins, and pick the unit: **mm** or **pt** (1/72 in).
3. Under **Apply to**, choose which pages to crop.
4. Click **Crop**.

| Apply to | Pages cropped |
| --- | --- |
| Current page | The page you are viewing |
| Selected pages | The pages selected in the thumbnails |
| Pages: | A range such as 1-3, 5, 8-10 |
| Odd pages / Even pages | Every odd or even page |
| All pages | The whole document |

### Draw the box instead

1. Click **Draw on page**, then drag a box on the page.
2. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to apply, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel and restore the earlier values.

The margin fields follow the box as you draw. The box is placed at the same position on every chosen page.

### Remove white margins

Click **Remove white margins** to fill the fields from the content of the current page, plus a small border. Tick **Detect each page separately** to trim every chosen page by its own content. Blank pages are left as they are.

## Resize pages

1. Choose **Tools ▸ Resize pages…**
2. Pick a **Page size** (A4, A3, Letter or Legal), or **Custom** and enter a width and height in **mm** or **in**.
3. Choose **Portrait** or **Landscape**.
4. Under **Content**, choose **Scale to fit** (keeps proportions, centred) or **Do not scale** (centred, and may be clipped).
5. Under **Apply to**, choose the pages, then click **Resize**.

> **Tip:** Press Undo if the result is not what you wanted. See [Undo and redo](#topic:undo-redo).
