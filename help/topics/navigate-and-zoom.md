## Go to a page

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Next / previous page | <kbd>Page Down</kbd> / <kbd>Page Up</kbd>, or the arrow buttons on the toolbar |
| First / last page | <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> |
| A given page | Type the number in the page box and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> [Show me](#showme:btn:page) |

You can also click a thumbnail in the sidebar, or use the **Hand** tool to drag the page around. [Show me](#showme:tool:hand)

## Zoom

| Command | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Zoom in | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>=</kbd> |
| Zoom out | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> |
| Fit page | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> |
| Actual size (100%) | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> |
| Fit width | **View ▸ Fit width** |

The zoom list on the toolbar offers **Fit width**, **Fit page** and 50% to 400%. [Show me](#showme:btn:zoom)

## Rotate the view

**View ▸ Rotate view left** and **View ▸ Rotate view right** turn the display only; the file is not changed. To rotate pages permanently, use the rotate buttons on the toolbar: they rotate the current page, or the pages selected in the thumbnails, and the change is saved with the file. [Show me](#showme:btn:rotr)

## Reading mode

Choose **View ▸ Reading mode** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>. The menus, toolbar and sidebar are hidden and the page fits the window width.

- Move the mouse to show a small control strip with **Previous page**, **Next page**, **Zoom out**, **Zoom in**, **Fit width**, **Fit page** and **Exit reading mode**. It hides again two seconds after the mouse stops.
- <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>: next page. <kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd>: previous page. <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd>: scroll.
- Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> to leave. Your previous zoom is restored.

## Full screen

**View ▸ Full screen** or <kbd>F11</kbd> fills the screen with the window. Press <kbd>F11</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to return.

> **Note:** If a tool or a selection is active, the first <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears it; press <kbd>Esc</kbd> again to leave reading mode or full screen.
