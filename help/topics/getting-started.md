ASH PDF Studio opens, reads and edits PDF files. Everything runs on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

## Open a file

Choose **File ▸ Open…** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>. You can also drop PDF files on the window.

## Add your first text

Pick the Text tool and click on the page. See [Add a text box](#topic:add-text-box).

> [!TIP]
> Press <kbd>F1</kbd> over any part of the window to open the help for it.
