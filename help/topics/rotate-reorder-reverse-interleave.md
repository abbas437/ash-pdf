# Rotate, move, reverse and interleave pages

## Rotate pages or only the view

| Command | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| [Rotate left](#showme:btn:rotl) / [Rotate right](#showme:btn:rotr) on the toolbar | Turns the selected pages (or the current page) and is saved with the file |
| View ▸ Rotate view left / right | Turns the display only; the file is not changed |

You can also press <kbd>[</kbd> or <kbd>]</kbd> with a thumbnail focused. Rotating pages can be undone.

## Move pages

1. Select the pages in [the thumbnails](#topic:page-thumbnails).
2. Right-click and choose **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to start** or **Move to end**.

Or drag the thumbnails to a new position. A command that would change nothing is greyed out.

## Reverse the page order

1. Choose Tools ▸ **Reverse page order…** (needs at least two pages).
2. Under Apply to, choose the selected pages or all pages.
3. Click **Reverse**.

> **Note:** With a selection, the selected pages swap among their own positions; other pages stay where they are.

## Interleave two scans

Use this when a double-sided document was scanned as two files: one of fronts, one of backs.

1. Choose Tools ▸ **Interleave pages from PDF…**.
2. Click **Choose PDF…** or pick an open document for the other sides.
3. Tick **Second file is in reverse order** if the backs were scanned last page first.
4. Click **Interleave**.

The result runs page 1 of the first file, page 1 of the second, page 2 of the first, and so on.

> **Important:** If the files have different page counts, the program warns you and adds the leftover pages at the end.
